## What the system is

MAGICK//AI OS treats AI as a **scribe, librarian, symbolic co-designer, planner and auditor**. It does not treat the model as a supernatural authority.

Three layers stay separate:
- **symbolic** — ritual language, sigils, archetypes, divination, synchronicity;
- **psychological** — attention, meaning, belief experiments, reflection;
- **operational** — goals, actions, evidence, journaling and review.

The user may interpret the symbolic layer however they choose. The AI does not present supernatural causation or prophecy as established fact.

## Core loop

`RAW → INTENT → METHOD → DESIGN → PERFORM → SEAL → OBSERVE → AUDIT → ITERATE`

The AI designs and records. The human decides and performs.
